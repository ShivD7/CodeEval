# CodeEval

Competitive programming practice for the Centennial CVI Computer Science Club.

* Rotates a Problem of the Week every Monday in Toronto time.
* Searches problems by title or topic.
* Filters problems by difficulty and completion status.
* Includes a separate club contest collection.
* Shows problem statements, constraints, and examples.
* Edits C++, Python, Java, and JavaScript in Monaco.
* Runs sample tests with output, errors, and timing.
* Grades submissions against sample and hidden tests through Judge0.
* Reports verdicts and passed test counts.
* Autosaves browser drafts by account, problem, and language.
* Saves submission code and results in private account history.
* Reopens saved submissions across devices.
* Tracks solved problems and overall progress.
* Supports Google sign-in or verified email accounts.
* Lets users change their display name and password.
* Adapts the workspace for smaller screens.

Saved grades track personal progress. They are written by the app client and are not a trusted contest scoreboard.
